Privacy Policy for Pieno Conveniente

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pieno Conveniente. The App is developed and managed by Gino Spezzano (the "Developer" or "We"). By using the App, you consent to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Google Account Login: If you log in using your Google account, we receive and collect your Google profile information, specifically your Name, Email address, and Google account unique identifier.
  • Supabase Auth Login: If you log in using Supabase Auth, we collect your authentication credentials and your Supabase user identifier.
  • User Preferences: If you are a registered user, we save your list of favorite gas stations and user preferences to synchronize them across your devices via our backend database.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: We use your device's GPS to detect your location to find and display nearby service stations. This data is collected on a non-continuous basis (only when you open the App or manually refresh the list). Location data is processed locally and is not continuously tracked or stored permanently on our servers.
  • Device Information: We generate a random device ID (UUID) upon installation. This identifier is completely pseudonymous and is used solely to manage API traffic limits (rate limiting) and ensure security.
  • Usage Data: We collect basic, anonymous data about how you interact with the App, such as features accessed and timestamps, to ensure proper technical functionality.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Supabase Database: We download fuel price data from the Supabase database via API to the device's local SQLite database.
  • Google Accounts & Supabase Auth: We receive your profile identifiers when you choose to authenticate using these third-party single sign-on services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Display Fuel Prices and Service Stations: To show you the most relevant fuel prices and nearby service stations based on your location.
  • User Authentication: To authenticate your account and manage your user profile.
  • Favorite Gas Stations: To save and synchronize your list of favorite gas stations.
  • App Functionality and Security: To prevent server abuse via rate limiting, improve features, display ads (via AdMob), and manage premium statistics for paid users.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Store: We use the Google Play Store for payment processing and to manage in-app purchases for the paid version of the App.
  • AdMob: We display ads via AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information from your device, such as your device ID and usage data, to serve targeted ads to users.
  • Supabase: We use Supabase to store and synchronize your list of favorite gas stations and to manage user authentication.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

  • Location Data: We retain location data only while the App is open and actively performing a search.
  • Device Information: The random device ID is stored as long as the App remains installed on your device.
  • User Data: Your profile information and list of favorite gas stations are stored as long as you have an active account.
  • How to Delete Your Data: In compliance with Google Play policies and GDPR, you can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. You can do this directly inside the App through the "Delete Account" option in the settings menu, or by contacting the Developer via email at [La Tua Email di Contatto]. Upon request, your data will be permanently deleted from our databases within 7 days.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include encryption in transit and secure database access controls.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to access, correct, port, or request the deletion of the personal data we hold about you. Since the App processes data under GDPR guidelines for European users, you can object to data processing or manage your ad tracking preferences at any time through the built-in consent banner.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take immediate steps to delete that data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, app features, or regulatory requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy within the App.
